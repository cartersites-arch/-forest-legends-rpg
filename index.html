A QUICK DRIVER BRIEFING

Keep the crew
rolling.

  1. Lead the convoy.Tap the left or right arrow to change lanes. Beverly follows your path a moment later, so leave room for the whole truck.
  2. Watch the road.Logs, washouts and rocks damage either vehicle. A hazard can hit both rigs.
  3. Pick up a little luck.Golden permits and compasses restore up to 18% convoy integrity. Either vehicle can collect them.
  4. Make some noise.The center button sounds the air horn. It is just for fun—it does not clear hazards.

You can also tap or swipe the road. On a keyboard: ← → or A/D to steer, Space to honk, P or Esc to pause.

On iPhone, open the hosted game in Safari. Use Share → Add to Home Screen for a full-screen shortcut. Keep this browser’s data to keep your saves.
